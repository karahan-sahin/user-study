Sign Language Study

Before you begin

Please read this short note. It explains how to give your ratings.

Judge every question separately. Each page shows one video and asks six different questions. Answer each question on its own, based only on what that question asks. Please do not let your impression of one aspect decide your answers to the others.
  • 📺 Each video is independent. Rate it by itself. Do not compare it with earlier videos, and do not try to work out how a video was made. Different videos may use different avatars, styles or quality.
  • ⚖ Separate aspects can differ. A video can look sharp but be hard to understand, move smoothly but carry the wrong meaning, or look blurry yet be easy to follow. Rate each aspect on its own merits.

The six questions

  • 1
    How easy is it to understand? How easily you can follow the signing, whatever the English sentence says.
  • 2
    Does it convey the same meaning as the English text? Compare the meaning of the signing with the English sentence shown above the video. Ignore how the video looks.
  • 3
    Quality of the translation (grammar and style). Whether the signing is grammatical, well formed and natural as a translation, not just whether single signs are correct.
  • 4
    Grammar only. Only the grammar: is the order of the signs and the structure of the sentence correct BSL? Ignore the meaning, the choice of vocabulary and how the avatar looks.
  • 5
    Visual quality of the avatar. Sharpness, artifacts, realism of the person and hands. Ignore what is being signed.
  • 6
    Naturalness of the motion. Whether the movement is smooth and human-like rather than stiff or robotic. Ignore the meaning of what is signed.

There are no right or wrong answers. You can watch each video as many times as you like. Please answer honestly, using the full 1–5 scale.
