Sign Language Study
Introduction

Welcome to the Duet BSL Translation Study

Thank you for participating. This study evaluates how well short sign language videos translate English sentences. Your responses will help improve computational sign language translation.

  • 📺 For each example you will read an English sentence, watch one or more sign language translations of it, and answer three short questions about each translation on a 5-point scale.
  • 🕐 The study takes less than 10 minutes to complete.
  • 👤 First we will ask one short question about your signing background. No sign language knowledge is required to participate.

Participation is voluntary and anonymous. You may stop at any time. By clicking Start Study you agree to participate.
